    How to use

    Turning the cube

    Swipe a coloured sticker to turn its row or column. You can also use the face buttons or keyboard; each move turns a layer 90°.

    • Swipe a sticker — turn that row or column in the swipe direction
    • Drag the dark background — orbit the camera without turning a layer
    • UDLRFB — turn that face clockwise
    • MES — turn a middle slice
    • Shift + letter — turn counter‑clockwise (prime)
    • XYZ — rotate the whole cube
    • Space — new scramble  ·  Backspace — undo

    The button pad also has wide turns (lowercase u r f…) that move two layers at once.

    Notation

    U up · D down · L left · R right · F front · B back.

    A plain letter is 90° clockwise (looking at that face). ′ is counter‑clockwise, ² is a 180° turn.

    Timer

    After a scramble the timer arms. It starts on your first move and stops the instant the cube is solved. Times are kept for the session.
